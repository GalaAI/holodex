Tray menu as pages, 2026-10-27

What changed
- The tray menu is one panel that turns pages. A row with a chevron (Grab from the screen, Spell Orb, Settings,
  and each Settings group) shows its page in the same panel; every page under home starts with a Back row
  (Back, or Back to Settings) and its eyebrow. Esc, Backspace and Left go back a page; Esc on home closes.
- It opens centered above the tray icon, 8px clear of the taskbar, kept inside the work area; its bottom edge
  stays put when a page changes height. Every page is the same width, so a turn never moves it sideways.
- Moving the pointer never closes it. Page rows, Back and setting rows (speed, mouse, pointer look, shape,
  tilt, size, the toggles, VR mode and fixes, Refresh the pointer, the pointer log) keep it open. It closes on
  a click outside it, Esc on home, another click on the icon, or a row that opens something or casts.
- PhosphorTrayMenu.cs replaces PhosphorMenuLayout, PhosphorMenuFrame and the old PhosphorTrayMenu; TrayProbe
  and SurfaceCapture turn pages instead of opening submenus.

Checked
- Build: 0 warnings, 0 errors.
- Offscreen render of every page (pages-sheet.png; menu*.png and menu-tray*.png in docs/shots/as-built).
- Live, through the pipe (tray:shoot, menu shown for a moment, no pointer or keys): icon 1642,1032 32x48,
  panel 1443,718 430x306 on home (centered on the icon), Settings/Mouse speed turned to 1443,733 430x291 with
  the bottom edge kept at 1024; checks=pass (inside the work area, clear of the icon, no clipped labels).
