Holodex and Hivemind Sessions, 2026-10-27 (all offscreen: nothing was shown on the desktop)

What it is
- Holodex and Hivemind stay two apps. Hivemind keeps the Sessions (rooms); Holodex reads and writes them through
  Hivemind's own MCP tools under its existing sign-in (runtime.read lists and reads, runtime.execute posts; no new
  sign-in was needed).
- The Session tab's target chip lists the agents, then the six newest Hivemind sessions. Picking a session shows its
  transcript as cards (you, each agent with its model, each connected client by name), follows new messages every
  10 s while it is open, and Send becomes "Reply in session". A reply posts as a client message from Omniwand and
  starts no agent. Picking an agent again returns to the Syndicate thread. "Open in Hivemind" opens the web app's
  Sessions view (it addresses views, not single rooms).

Gaps fixed in the same change
- The control pipe started before the app was built; it now starts last in the constructor.
- The first tray capture after a launch failed: the menu closed with AppFocusChange because a background process
  cannot take the foreground. TrayProbe records the close reason and retries once, as a real click would succeed.
- Long HUD answers were capped at 260 device pixels (173 logical at 150%); the cap is now 520 logical, DPI-scaled,
  at most half the work area, and model markdown (**bold**, # headings, bullets) shows as plain text.
- Holodex said "Signed out" on its work-orders card when Hivemind connected before its window existed; it now
  catches up when its handle is created.
